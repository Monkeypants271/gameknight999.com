# GameKnight999.com — Project Spec, Version 1

## Purpose

Give readers who discover `GameKnight999.com` through the books a friendly, current place to land. The site will explain that the Gameknight999 Minecraft server is closed, celebrate the community that grew around it, and invite young readers to explore the books and write stories of their own.

## Audience

- Readers of the Gameknight999 books, especially kids
- Parents, teachers, and librarians who visit with them
- Former players who want to revisit the server's story

## Site structure

A simple, responsive single-page website with clear links to relevant pages on MarkCheverton.com.

### 1. Welcome

- Headline: **“The server is closed. The adventures aren't.”**
- A short welcome from Mark
- A clear statement that the Gameknight999 server has closed
- A warm, brief thank-you to the players and staff who made it a memorable community
- Use the sunrise Minecraft landscape as the main welcome image (see Image assets below).

### 2. The server's story

- A short personal remembrance of the server
- Explain that it gave Mark a chance to meet readers and play with them
- Keep the tone appreciative and honest; don't suggest the server might return
- The castle exterior may be used as a supporting image here or beside the books section. Keep text separate from the detailed image or place it on a solid overlay.

### 3. The books

- A prominent call to action linking to the Gameknight999 books page on MarkCheverton.com
- Suggested button text: **“Explore the Gameknight999 Books”**
- The books page will hold book details, covers, reading order, and purchase links. This site won't duplicate that material.

### 4. Write your own adventure

- Encourage kids to imagine and write their own Minecraft stories
- A prominent link to the fan fiction page on MarkCheverton.com
- Suggested button text: **“Write Your Own Story”**
- Keep the invitation encouraging and age-appropriate, without collecting submissions on this site

### 5. Footer

- Link to MarkCheverton.com
- Copyright and brief site attribution
- No server status widget or other server address

## Design direction

- A playful Minecraft-inspired look that feels welcoming to middle-grade readers
- Use blocky visual details as accents, while keeping text easy to read
- Make the closed-server message clear without making it the whole mood of the site
- Use large, obvious buttons for the books and writing links
- Work well on phones, tablets, and desktop screens
- Keep the site fast and lightweight

## Out of scope for Version 1

- Accounts, forums, comments, chat, or newsletters
- Hosting or collecting fan fiction
- Rebuilding server functionality
- Recommending or linking to other Minecraft servers
- Duplicating the books catalog already on MarkCheverton.com

## Success criteria

A young reader arriving from a book can quickly understand that the server has closed, find the Gameknight999 books, and discover where to write a story of their own. A former player can also find a brief, sincere acknowledgment of the community.
